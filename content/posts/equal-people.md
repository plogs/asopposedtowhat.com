---
title: "The People Who Wanted to Be Equal"
date: 2026-09-19T19:49:09-07:00
draft: false
---

In the land of the twenty-first century, people saw
differences in the world and said that means life is unfair.

So they made their speeches, passed some laws, and took money
from the rich and gave to the poor.

But that was not enough.

They surgically shortened the shins of the tall and lengthened
the legs of the short.

But that was not enough.

They removed an eye from those with two, and gave it to the blind.

But that was not enough.

And on and on it went.
Difference after difference was fixed, advantage after advantage
removed,
until one day at last, everyone was equal---and
all diversity in the world was gone.

Then the people who saw the differences in the world and said that
means life is unfair all died.

But no one noticed, no one cared.

Life was still unfair.
